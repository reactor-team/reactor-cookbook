# Rough storyboard panels for "Desert signal"

Generate one image per shot with the same image tool used for `public/references/prompts.json`,
save them here as `01.png` … `05.png`, and set each shot's `image` in `lib/storyboard.ts` to
`/boards/0N.png`. The photos in `public/references/` stay as the cast, location, and prop
references; these panels are only what the board looks like before it moves.

Shared style line, prepend to every prompt:

> Rough hand-drawn storyboard panel, pencil and light grey marker on off-white paper, loose
> confident lines, minimal shading, a few arrows for camera movement, no color, no text, no
> watermark, 16:9 panel with a thin hand-ruled border.

1. **An empty frequency.** Wide establishing shot of a slender lattice radio antenna with guy
   wires beside a small concrete hut on pale dunes at dawn; low sun on the left; a single arrow
   pushing in toward the hut to mark a slow aerial move. No people.
2. **The traveler.** Low tracking shot: a woman in a field jacket with a shoulder strap walks
   across the dunes toward the distant station, long shadow stretching behind her; a sideways
   arrow along the bottom edge for the track.
3. **Searching for a signal.** Extreme close-up of a hand turning the knurled tuning knob of a
   weathered shortwave receiver; the station a soft blur in the background; small motion lines
   on the dial.
4. **Is anyone out there.** Close-up of the woman lifting her eyes from the receiver toward the
   horizon, lips slightly parted mid-line; a small arrow pushing in on her face.
5. **The other side.** Wide shot: the antenna and hut on the dunes, the woman small in the
   foreground holding the receiver; faint lights sketched beyond the far dunes; an arrow pulling
   back from the scene.
